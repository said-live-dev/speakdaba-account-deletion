SpeakDaba - Account Deletion

SpeakDaba users can delete their accounts directly from the app by following these steps:

  1. Open the SpeakDaba app.
  2. Go to your profile and open Edit Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the request and enter your password when asked.

Deleting your account removes your account and associated data, including profile information, conversations, messages, and related user data, subject to any data that must be retained for legal or security reasons.

If you cannot access the app, contact the SpeakDaba developer to request account deletion.
